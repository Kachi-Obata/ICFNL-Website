Frequently Asked Questions

Beware of Fraud

If you receive suspicious emails pretending to be from banks, courts, or senior Government officials, verify the sender's website. Scammers often use free email services.

Criminals who run scams send emails from free public email services and pretend that these emails come from Banks, Courts and senior Government officials.

Please note that Banks (particularly the Central Bank of Nigeria) do not conduct official business using free public email services.

Common Scams

  • Fake bank websites mimicking real institutions.
  • Fraudulent requests for money transfers via cryptocurrency or Western Union.
  • Emails claiming inheritance from unknown relatives.
  • Job scams requesting upfront payments.
  • False legal services requiring bogus certificates.

Always verify claims through official channels. Contact us for guidance.
